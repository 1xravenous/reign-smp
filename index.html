ABOUT REIGN SMP

Reign SMP is a private Minecraft survival realm divided among seven kingdoms. Each kingdom has its own territory, culture, architecture, ambitions, and history.

The world of Reign is shaped by the people who inhabit it. Kingdoms rise, borders change, alliances are formed, and rivalries develop throughout the realm.

The seven kingdoms are connected by a shared world, but each follows its own path.

HISTORY OF THE REALM

THE LORE OF REIGN

I

THE UNCLAIMED LAND

Before the seven kingdoms were established, the land that would become Reign was vast and largely unexplored. Mountains divided the territories, forests covered the wilderness, and ancient structures hinted that others had lived there long before the current inhabitants.

II

THE FOUNDING OF REIGN

As the first settlers arrived, they began establishing permanent homes across the realm. Instead of forming a single nation, the settlers divided into seven distinct groups. These groups eventually became the seven kingdoms of Reign.

III

THE SEVEN KINGDOMS

Valthera, Eldoria, Veylcrest, Aetherion, Dravenfall, Solmire, and Verdantis established their own territories. Each kingdom developed different traditions, architecture, resources, and ambitions while remaining connected to the same world.

IV

THE AGE OF KINGDOMS

With the seven territories established, civilization spread throughout Reign. Roads connected distant settlements, castles rose above the landscape, resources became valuable, and relationships between kingdoms began to shape the history of the realm.

V

THE BALANCE OF REIGN

No kingdom controls the entire realm. Every kingdom possesses something the others may need. Trade, cooperation, competition, and political decisions determine the balance between the seven powers.

VI

THE STORY CONTINUES

Reign has no predetermined ending. The future of the realm depends on the actions of its kingdoms and inhabitants. New settlements may appear, alliances may change, and the history of the seven kingdoms will continue to be written inside the world of Reign SMP.
